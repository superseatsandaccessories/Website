## Where we come from

Our roots are in Pawar Seat Covers, started in 1958 by Mr. Sadashiv Pawar in Nana Peth, Pune, repairing and making bicycle seats. Riders from across Maharashtra came for custom seats for the Royal Enfield Bullet, Matchless, Triumph, BSA, Yezdi and Java.

The business grew into trading and manufacturing for the industry, and became an official seat supplier for Bajaj two-wheelers under the name Vijay Seat Manufacturing Company.

Today, as Super Seats and Accessories on JM Road, we make and trade in seats, seat covers, helmets, stainless steel accessories, grip covers and matting. We focus on wholesale: consistent quality at competitive prices, for riders across Pune and beyond.
